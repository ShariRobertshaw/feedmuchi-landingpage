Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 July 2026

Who we are

Muchi is a gut health app built by Shari Robertshaw, based in Bath, UK. You can contact us at hello@feedmuchi.com.

What data we collect

When you create an account:

  • Your email address
  • A password (stored securely, we never see it in plain text)

When you use the app:

  • Foods you log and their estimated fibre content
  • Photos you take or upload of your meals — these are used only to identify food items and are not stored permanently
  • Spoken meal descriptions, if you use voice logging — the recording is sent for transcription and is not retained
  • Barcodes you scan
  • Your daily fibre totals and plant variety counts
  • The name you give your Muchi pet, and Muchi's mood/progress state

Automatically:

  • A random device identifier, used only to prevent abuse of our systems (rate-limiting). This isn't linked to analytics and isn't used to track your behaviour.
  • Optional analytics, via PostHog — see below.

Optional analytics

Analytics is off by default. The first time you visit Home after signing in, we ask if you'd like to turn it on. You can change your choice at any time in Account, and using Muchi doesn't require it either way.

If you turn it on, we send a limited set of usage events — like which screen you're on, which way you logged a meal (photo, barcode, voice, etc.), and whether an action succeeded or failed — using a temporary identifier that only lasts for that session. It isn't linked to your account or email, and we don't use it to build a profile of you or track you across separate visits.

Analytics never sees the actual content of what you log: no food names, photos, audio, transcripts, fibre or nutrition numbers, or your Muchi's mood. It's purely "which screen, which button" — not "what did you eat."

We use PostHog for this, with data processed in the EU. Events are kept for 7 years. You can turn analytics off any time in Account, which stops any further collection.

What we don't collect

  • We never collect your weight, body measurements, or calorie intake
  • We never sell your data to anyone
  • We never use your data for advertising

How we use your data

We use your data to:

  • Run the app and save your food logs across sessions
  • Identify food items from photos, barcodes, or voice descriptions you submit
  • Show you your fibre progress and Muchi's mood
  • Improve the app based on how people use it (only if you've opted into analytics)
  • Send you account-related emails (signup confirmation, password reset)
  • Keep the app secure and prevent abuse

Who we share data with

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to run Muchi:

Supabase
Stores your account and food log data securely. Based in the US with EU data transfer safeguards in place. supabase.com/privacy

Resend
Sends transactional emails (signup confirmation, password reset). resend.com/privacy

OpenRouter / Google Gemini
Your meal photos or typed/spoken descriptions are sent to an AI model to identify food items. This content isn't stored by us after analysis. openrouter.ai/privacy

Open Food Facts
Barcode scans are looked up against Open Food Facts' database to identify products. world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

USDA FoodData Central
Used as a fallback for food and nutrition lookups when Open Food Facts doesn't have a match. This is a US government database; no account or personal data is sent to it beyond the search term.

PostHog
Optional, session-only usage analytics, hosted in the EU — only active if you've turned it on. posthog.com/privacy

We do not share your data with anyone else.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your food log data
  • Correct any inaccurate data
  • Withdraw consent to analytics at any time, without affecting your ability to use the app
  • Complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@feedmuchi.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely by Supabase. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We use row-level security so users can only access their own data.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete all your personal data within 30 days — this covers your account, food logs, and profile information held in Supabase.

Optional analytics events sent to PostHog are kept separately, for 7 years, since they aren't linked to your account and so aren't deleted as part of account deletion.

Cookies and local storage

The Muchi app doesn't use cookies. On the web app, we use your browser's local storage to remember your analytics preference on that device — this isn't a tracking cookie, and it's specific to the browser or device you set it on. You can change your analytics choice any time in Account.

Notifications

If you enable food-log reminders, Muchi schedules these locally on your device. We don't send any data to a third party to deliver them.

Children

Muchi is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this policy we'll notify you by email. The latest version is always at feedmuchi.com/privacy.
