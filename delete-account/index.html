Delete your Muchi account

You can permanently delete your Muchi account and the personal data linked to it.

Deleting your account removes your profile, saved meals, meal photos, and progress. This cannot be undone.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Muchi app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when asked.

You’ll be signed out once deletion starts. You won’t be able to sign back in with that account.

What we delete

When you delete your account, we remove:

  • Your Muchi account and sign-in details
  • Your meal log and related progress
  • Meal photos stored with your account

Some records may remain briefly in backups or operational logs while deletion completes. We aim to finish removing personal data within 30 days, except where we’re legally required to keep something longer.

If you can’t use the app

Email us at hello@feedmuchi.com from the address on your Muchi account, and ask us to delete it. Include:

  • The email address on your account
  • A clear request to delete your Muchi account

We’ll confirm when the request is complete.
