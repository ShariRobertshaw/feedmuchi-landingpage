Muchi Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy is provided separately from Muchi's general Privacy Policy, as required by the Washington My Health My Data Act (MHMDA). It applies specifically to "consumer health data" as defined by that law, and describes what we collect, why, and the rights available to you. It does not contain any promotional or marketing content.

This policy applies to all users of Muchi, and specifically extends these rights to consumers in Washington State as required by law.

1. What Consumer Health Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information that could be considered consumer health data:

  • Dietary and nutrition information: meals, foods, and plant varieties you log, including fibre intake estimates and other nutrition values (such as macros) returned with food lookups, derived from that logging
  • Photos of food you submit for automatic meal analysis
  • Voice you submit for audio-based meal logging — we send the recording for transcription and do not keep the audio file; only the transcript and resulting meal analysis are stored
  • Free-text descriptions of meals you enter manually
  • Barcode scans you submit to look up packaged foods
  • Inferred wellbeing indicators: your Muchi's emotional/health state, which is derived from your logged fibre and plant intake against your daily goals

We do not collect or infer: weight, body measurements, medical diagnoses, menstrual/reproductive health data, or biometric identifiers. Muchi is not a calorie-counting product — macros may be stored with logged foods from nutrition lookups, but they are not used as a primary goal or scoring system.

2. Why We Collect This Data

We collect and use consumer health data to:

  • Calculate your daily fibre intake and plant-diversity score
  • Power the core app mechanic (your Muchi's emotional state)
  • Provide meal analysis via photo, barcode, voice, or text processing
  • Track your progress toward your fibre and plant-variety goals
  • Show you fibre and related nutrition estimates for the meals you log
  • Provide customer support when you contact us about your data or logs

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Sources of This Data

We collect consumer health data:

  • Directly from you, when you log meals via photo, barcode, voice, or text
  • From our service providers, who help us process what you submit (see Section 4)

We do not purchase or acquire consumer health data about you from any third party.

4. Who We Share This Data With

We share consumer health data only with the service providers necessary to operate Muchi:

  • Meal analysis — photos and text descriptions (Google Gemini via OpenRouter) — identifying foods from photos and typed descriptions
  • Meal analysis — voice (Whisper via OpenRouter) — transcribing spoken meal descriptions before analysis; the audio file is not retained by Muchi
  • Food database lookups (USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts) — matching foods and barcodes to nutrition/fibre data
  • Authentication (Supabase, Apple, Google) — account sign-in
  • Infrastructure (Cloudflare) — hosting and app delivery

Optional in-app analytics (PostHog, EU region) is covered in Muchi's general Privacy Policy. If you opt in, analytics events may include how you logged a meal (for example photo, barcode, voice, or text), but not meal contents, fibre totals, plant lists, photos, audio, or transcripts. Analytics is a separate opt-in from consumer health data consent.

We do not share consumer health data with data brokers, advertisers, or affiliates for marketing purposes. We do not sell consumer health data.

Each of these providers is bound by contract to use your data only to provide their service to us, not for their own independent purposes.

5. Your Rights

If you are a Washington resident (or wherever else these rights apply to you), you have the right to:

  • Know & access — confirm whether we're collecting, sharing, or selling your consumer health data, and request a copy, including a list of third parties who've received it
  • Delete — request deletion of your consumer health data from our systems and instruct our service providers to do the same
  • Withdraw consent — revoke any consent you've previously given for collection or sharing of your consumer health data, at any time, including via the Settings toggle in the app
  • Appeal — if we deny a request, you can appeal the decision and will receive a written response

To exercise access, deletion, or appeal rights, email hello@feedmuchi.com with the subject line "Consumer Health Data Request." We will respond within 45 days, with a possible one-time 45-day extension if reasonably necessary. Requests are free up to twice per year.

You can withdraw consumer health data consent at any time in Account / Settings. Declining or withdrawing consent means we stop collecting consumer health data, so features that need that data — including meal logging and analysis — will not work until you give consent again. The rest of the app remains available. Exercising access, deletion, or appeal rights will not otherwise reduce the features available to you.

6. Consent

Before we collect the consumer health data described in Section 1, we ask for your separate, affirmative opt-in consent in the app. You can decline. If you decline, meal logging and analysis stay off until you opt in.

You can withdraw that consent at any time in Account / Settings. After withdrawal, we stop collecting new consumer health data. Existing logs remain until you delete them or request deletion (see Section 5 and Section 9).

Separately, we ask for your affirmative opt-in before enabling in-app usage tracking (analytics). Analytics consent is independent of consumer health data consent. You can decline or turn off analytics without affecting meal logging, provided you have given consumer health data consent.

For access, deletion, or appeal requests, use the email contact in Section 5. To delete your account and stored consumer health data, see Section 5 and Section 9.

7. Data Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your consumer health data, including encryption in transit and access controls limiting who can view raw logs.

8. Geofencing

We do not use geofencing technology to identify or track users near health care facilities, nor do we send targeted content based on proximity to such locations.

9. Data Retention

Meal photos are stored so you can review your past logged meals within the app. They are retained for as long as your account is active.

Voice audio used for meal logging is sent for transcription and is not stored in our database. Transcripts and the resulting meal analysis are retained with your food log for as long as your account is active.

Food logs, plant-variety counts, fibre totals, nutrition values stored with logged foods, and your Muchi's derived state are kept for as long as your account is active.

If you delete your account (or submit a consumer health data deletion request), we delete this data, including stored meal photos, from our systems within 30 days, and instruct our service providers to do the same.
