Muchi Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy is provided separately from Muchi's general Privacy Policy, as required by the Washington My Health My Data Act (MHMDA). It applies specifically to "consumer health data" as defined by that law, and describes what we collect, why, and the rights available to you. It does not contain any promotional or marketing content.

This policy applies to all users of Muchi, and specifically extends these rights to consumers in Washington State as required by law.

1. What Consumer Health Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information that could be considered consumer health data:

  • Dietary and nutrition information: meals, foods, and plant varieties you log, including fibre intake estimates derived from that logging
  • Photos of food you submit for automatic meal analysis
  • Voice recordings you submit for audio-based meal logging (transcribed via our processor and not retained in Muchi's database; only the resulting meal description is stored)
  • Free-text descriptions of meals you enter manually
  • Barcode scans you submit for food identification
  • Inferred wellbeing indicators: your Muchi's emotional/health state, which is derived from your logged fibre and plant intake against your daily goals

Meal macros (including calories) may be stored when you log meals. We do not collect or infer: weight, medical diagnoses, menstrual/reproductive health data, or biometric identifiers.

2. Why We Collect This Data

We collect and use consumer health data to:

  • Calculate your daily fibre intake and plant-diversity score
  • Power the core app mechanic (your Muchi's emotional state)
  • Provide meal analysis via automated photo/audio/text processing
  • Track your progress toward your fibre and plant-variety goals
  • Improve the accuracy of our food-recognition and analysis features
  • Provide customer support when you contact us about your data or logs

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Sources of This Data

We collect consumer health data:

  • Directly from you, when you log meals via photo, voice, or text
  • From our service providers, who help us process what you submit (see Section 4)

We do not purchase or acquire consumer health data about you from any third party.

4. Who We Share This Data With

We share consumer health data only with the service providers necessary to operate Muchi:

  • AI/meal analysis processor (Gemini via our meal analysis stack/OpenRouter) — analyzing food photos and text descriptions
  • Voice transcription processor (Whisper via OpenRouter) — transcribing audio to text (audio not retained)
  • Food database lookups (USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts) — matching foods to nutrition/fibre data and resolving barcode scans
  • Authentication (Supabase, Apple, Google) — account sign-in
  • Infrastructure (Cloudflare) — hosting and app delivery
  • Analytics, only if you opt in (PostHog, EU region) — understanding app usage patterns. Analytics may receive entry method (e.g., photo vs text vs voice vs barcode) but not meal contents. Analytics has its own separate consent. See our general Privacy Policy for more details.

We do not share consumer health data with data brokers, advertisers, or affiliates for marketing purposes. We do not sell consumer health data.

Each of these providers is bound by contract to use your data only to provide their service to us, not for their own independent purposes.

5. Your Rights

If you are a Washington resident (or wherever else these rights apply to you), you have the right to:

  • Know & access — confirm whether we're collecting, sharing, or selling your consumer health data, and request a copy, including a list of third parties who've received it
  • Delete — request deletion of your consumer health data from our systems and instruct our service providers to do the same
  • Withdraw consent — revoke any consent you've previously given for collection or sharing of your consumer health data, at any time
  • Appeal — if we deny a request, you can appeal the decision and will receive a written response

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@feedmuchi.com with the subject line "Consumer Health Data Request." We will respond within 45 days, with a possible one-time 45-day extension if reasonably necessary. Requests are free up to twice per year.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights. Note that if you decline or withdraw consent to collect consumer health data, meal logging features that require sending health data will be unavailable, as we cannot provide those features without processing that data.

6. Consent

Before consumer health data (meal photos, voice recordings, descriptions, barcode scans, and derived fibre/plant data) is collected, leaves your device, or is written to our backend, Muchi will ask for your separate, affirmative opt-in consent. You may decline this consent, in which case meal logging features that require sending health data will remain unavailable.

You can withdraw your health data consent at any time in the app's Settings. If you withdraw consent, no further consumer health data will be collected or transmitted, and you may request deletion of previously collected data as described in Section 5.

Separately, we ask for your affirmative opt-in consent before enabling in-app usage tracking (analytics). Analytics consent is independent of health data consent. You can decline analytics without affecting your ability to use Muchi's core features, provided you have granted health data consent.

You can withdraw analytics consent at any time using the contact method in Section 5, or in Settings if available. To request deletion of your account and stored consumer health data, see Section 5.

7. Data Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your consumer health data, including encryption in transit and access controls limiting who can view raw logs.

8. Geofencing

We do not use geofencing technology to identify or track users near health care facilities, nor do we send targeted content based on proximity to such locations.

9. Data Retention

Meal photos are stored so you can review your past logged meals within the app. They are retained for as long as your account is active.

Food logs, plant-variety counts, fibre totals, and your Muchi's derived state are kept for as long as your account is active.

If you delete your account (or submit a consumer health data deletion request), we delete this data, including stored meal photos, from our systems within 30 days, and instruct our service providers to do the same.
